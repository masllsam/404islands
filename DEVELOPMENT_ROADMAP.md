# 🏗️ Project Archipelago - Realistic Development Roadmap

## Executive Summary

**Project Archipelago** transforms your voxel modeling expertise into a focused, revenue-generating digital art platform. The realistic approach reduces scale from 1000 to **404 islands** for practical development while maintaining artistic integrity.

**Mission:** Create the world's most immersive browser-based nature simulation, where 404 unique islands evolve with living weather, seasonal cycles, and catastrophic events - all controllable by user stewards.

## 🚨 Critical Path Risk Mitigation

### **Primary Risk:** Oracle Free Tier Limitations
- **Monitoring:** Weekly usage reviews (Weeks 2, 6, 10, 14...)
- **Plan B:** Cloudflare CDN as traffic absorber
- **Worst Case:** $50/month traffic overage acceptable

### **Secondary Risk:** Web Audio API Complexity
- **Fallback:** Pre-generated sound samples as backup
- **Optimization:** Browser testing across 10+ devices
- **Success Gate:** 85% device compatibility target

### **Tertiary Risk:** Pixel Art Fulfillment Scaling**
- **Pilot-First:** Start with 50 units to validate process
- **Partner Diversification:** 3+ fulfillment providers
- **Cost Buffer:** Allocate 20% contingency in fulfillment budget
